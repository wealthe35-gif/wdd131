Introduction to Design Principles

Design is the process of creating a plan or solution to a problem. It involves understanding the needs of the user, the context in which the solution will be used, and the constraints that must be considered. Design can take many forms, including graphic design, product design, and web design.

Color Contrast

Color contrast is an important aspect of design that affects the readability and accessibility of content. High contrast between text and background colors can improve legibility, while low contrast can make it difficult for users to read and understand the information presented.
